# Code of conduct

JevLab welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project discussions.

- Critique ideas and code, not people. Explain disagreements with concrete examples.
- Ask questions in good faith and leave room for people to learn or correct mistakes.
- Respect people's privacy, time, and boundaries. Do not share private information without permission.
- Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, and repeated personal attacks are not acceptable.

The maintainer may ask participants to change their behavior, remove content,
close a discussion, or restrict participation. Serious or repeated misconduct
can result in a ban from project spaces. Maintainers are held to the same standards.

For a project moderation concern that is safe to discuss publicly, mention
[@javsanesq](https://github.com/javsanesq) on the relevant thread. Do not repeat
private details or abusive content to draw attention to it. GitHub also provides
[reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
for abuse and privacy violations, including concerns involving the maintainer.
